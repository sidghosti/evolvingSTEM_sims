Bead Experiment Simulation

A simulated version of the EvolvingSTEM bead experiment. Grow a culture of bacteria and watch as they divide (binary fission). Every now and then, a mutant will arise (click on "Reveal Mutants" to see them as different colors!). These mutants can then rise or fall in frequency due to the factors of selection (environmental adaptation) or drift (random change). Add beads to provide a surface for biofilm-forming mutants to attach to and observe how this selective force causes the mutant frequencies change over multiple passages. And most important... have fun!

⚙️ Advanced settings

Per-lineage parameters, mostly for instructor use. Attaching to a surface (biofilm-forming capacity) is live: each tick a cell touches the edge of the culture dish, it has that % chance of sticking there for good. Other bacteria don't count as a surface yet, so a biofilm can only form as a single layer along the wall.
